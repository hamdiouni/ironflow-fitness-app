| **LEG-01** | **P0 HIGH** | Bundled fonts (`Inter.ttf`, `SpaceGrotesk.ttf`) lack bundled `OFL.txt` license files and `LicenseRegistry` registration | `assets/fonts/` contains only `Inter.ttf` (876 KB) and `SpaceGrotesk.ttf` (136 KB).
