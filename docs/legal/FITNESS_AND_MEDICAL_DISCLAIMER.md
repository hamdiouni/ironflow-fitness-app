1. **Before Starting**: Prior to beginning any new exercise regimen, physical conditioning protocol, strength training program, or nutritional modification provided or suggested by IronFlow, you must consult with a licensed medical physician.
2. **Pre-Existing Conditions**: If you have a personal or family history of heart disease, hypertension, cardiovascular conditions, spinal injuries, joint disorders, diabetes, metabolic conditions, respiratory illnesses, or are pregnant or nursing, medical clearance is strictly required before undertaking any physical training suggested by the app.
3. **Acute Symptoms**: If at any time while exercising you experience:
   - Acute, sharp, radiating, or chest pain
   - Dizziness, vertigo, or lightheadedness
   - Shortness of breath or respiratory distress
   - Irregular heartbeat or palpitations

   **STOP EXERCISING IMMEDIATELY AND CALL YOUR EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES OR CONSULT A PHYSICIAN WITHOUT DELAY.**

## 3. Anatomical Body Map & Recovery Estimator Limitations

The IronFlow interactive 2D Vector Body Map provides **mathematical approximations** of muscle recovery based on:
- Time elapsed since recorded workout completion.
- Volume loads (sets $\times$ reps $\times$ weight).
- General exponential decay algorithms and user-reported Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS).

**These estimates do NOT reflect:**
- Actual clinical soft-tissue healing.
- Tendon, ligament, or joint recovery state.
- Latent injury, inflammation, or neurological fatigue.
- Individual biochemical or hormonal recovery variance.

Users must always exercise sound athletic judgment and listen to their own bodily sensations rather than relying solely on graphical recovery visualizations.
