Direction Deep Dives Pros, Cons & Implementation Strategy

1. Slate Teal Refined

Lowest Risk • Medium Effort
"The Slate Teal palette already chosen, in its cleanest form: calm slate surfaces, one teal action color, and no extra borders or glow."
Strengths:
  • Preserves existing brand identity; feels like polish rather than a disruptive rebrand.
  • Direct 1:1 mapping to current AppThemeTokens and architecture.
  • Optimized for dark gym environments with minimal eye fatigue.
  • 100% WCAG AA contrast pass across both Dark and Light modes.
Trade-offs:
  • Least visually radical of the four directions.
  • Single primary accent requires disciplined secondary status colors.

2. Nordic Calm

Light-First • Large Effort
"A quiet, light, editorial look: lots of white space, black ink buttons, thin rules instead of boxes, and one sage green for good news."
Strengths:
  • Unmatched reading calm; eliminates tracker anxiety.
  • Excels on desktop web browsers and under daylight training.
  • Pure single-font Inter typography simplifies assets.
Trade-offs:
  • Significant departure from typical dark gym aesthetics.
  • Solid black ink buttons lack motivational color cueing.
  • 24 px padding increases vertical scrolling.

3. Kinetic Block

High-Density • Large Effort
"Bold and sporty: compact bento blocks, strong cobalt and amber color blocks, big scoreboard numbers, and square, confident buttons."
Strengths:
  • Maximum athletic energy; scoreboard numbers readable from across the gym.
  • Compact density displays more telemetry above the fold.
  • Square 4 px tap targets leave zero click ambiguity.
Trade-offs:
  • ALL-CAPS headings complicate localized strings.
  • Dual accents (cobalt + amber) require careful visual balance.
  • Requires rebuilding screens into multi-column bento grids.

4. Soft Layered

Approachable • Med-Large Effort
"Friendly and coaching-style: soft rounded sheets that float a little, capsule buttons, and gentle periwinkle and mint accents."
Strengths:
  • Encouraging and human; fits the AI Coach conversational tone.
  • 54 px capsule buttons provide superior thumb ergonomics.
  • Warm, harmonious adaptation between dark and light modes.
Trade-offs:
  • 26 px corner radii consume screen perimeter on small phones.
  • Subtle ambient shadows carry a small compositing cost.
  • Pastel tones feel less aggressive to heavy powerlifters.
