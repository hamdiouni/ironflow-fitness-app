## 🎯 How to Use

### Step 1: Open the App
```
http://localhost:8080
```

### Step 2: Navigate to AI Chat
Click on the AI Chat section in the app

### Step 3: Start Chatting
Send any message, for example:
- "Create a workout program for me"
- "What should I eat today?"
- "How can I improve my bench press?"
- "Am I making progress?"

### Step 4: Get Personalized Advice
The AI will respond with advice based on:
- Your profile (name, goals, fitness level, equipment)
- Your workout history (last 30 days)
- Your nutrition tracking (last 7 days)
- Your body measurements (last 30 days)
