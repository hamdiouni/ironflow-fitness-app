# Global AI Coach - User Guide

## Welcome to Your Personal AI Fitness Coach! 🎯

The Global AI Coach is your intelligent training partner that analyzes your workouts, nutrition, and body metrics to provide personalized coaching insights. Think of it as having a personal trainer who's always watching your progress and ready with actionable advice.

## What is the Global AI Coach?

The AI Coach is a smart feature that:
- **Analyzes your data** from workouts, meals, and body measurements
- **Identifies patterns** in your training and nutrition
- **Provides actionable insights** to help you reach your goals faster
- **Celebrates your wins** like PRs, volume records, and consistency streaks
- **Keeps you on track** with timely reminders and recommendations

### Key Features

✅ **Context-Specific Insights**: Different insights for Home, Workout, Nutrition, and Profile screens
✅ **Real-Time Feedback**: Instant celebration after completing workouts
✅ **Privacy-First**: All analysis happens on your device - your data never leaves your phone
✅ **Actionable Recommendations**: Specific numbers and clear next steps
✅ **Smart Caching**: Fast loading with intelligent background updates

## How It Works

### 1. Data Collection

The AI Coach analyzes:
- **Workouts** (last 30 days): Exercises, sets, reps, weights, volume trends
- **Nutrition** (last 7 days): Meals, macros, calorie intake, tracking consistency
- **Body Metrics** (last 30 days): Weight, measurements, trends over time

### 2. Insight Generation

The AI Coach looks for:
- **Progressive overload** patterns (are you getting stronger?)
- **Muscle group balance** (are you training all areas equally?)
- **Nutrition adherence** (are you hitting your macro targets?)
- **Consistency** (how often are you training and tracking?)
- **Goal alignment** (is your progress matching your goals?)

### 3. Personalized Recommendations

You'll receive:
- **2-3 insights** on each screen, prioritized by importance
- **Specific numbers** (e.g., "Your volume increased by 15%")
- **Clear action plans** (e.g., "Add 2 eggs to hit your protein target")
- **Celebration messages** for achievements (PRs, streaks, records)

## Where to Find Insights

## Quick Feedback After Workouts

### What is Quick Feedback?

After completing a workout, you'll see a celebration bottom sheet highlighting your achievements. This appears within 1 second and auto-dismisses after 10 seconds.

### What Gets Celebrated?

🏆 **Personal Records (PRs)**
- "🎉 New Personal Record! You just hit a new PR on Squat with 140kg!"

📈 **Volume Records**
- "💪 Volume Record Smashed! You lifted 3,200kg total volume today - that's 15% higher than your recent average!"

🔥 **Consistency Milestones**
- "🔥 One Week Strong! You've completed 7 days of training! You're building a solid foundation."

💪 **General Encouragement**
- "💪 Great Workout! You lifted 2,800kg total volume and you're on a 3-day streak. Keep it up!"

### How to Interact

- **Tap "View Detailed Insights"** to see more analysis (coming soon)
- **Tap "Dismiss"** to close the celebration
- **Swipe down** or **tap outside** to dismiss
- **Wait 10 seconds** and it auto-dismisses

## Understanding Your Insights

### Priority Levels

Insights are color-coded by importance:

🔴 **High Priority** (Urgent Action Needed)
- Workout reminders (3+ days without training)
- Protein deficit (below 80% of target)
- Calorie deviation (200+ kcal off target)
- PR achievements
- Volume records

🟡 **Medium Priority** (Important Recommendations)
- Nutrition tracking consistency
- Muscle group balance
- Deload suggestions
- Macro adherence

🟢 **Low Priority** (Informational)
- General summaries
- Workout counts
- Weight stability

### Reading an Insight

Each insight follows this format:

**[Icon] Title**
Analysis → Numbers → Action Plan

**Example:**
"📈 Progressive Overload Detected
Your training volume has increased by 15% (from 2,500kg to 2,875kg) over the last 30 days. Keep gradually increasing weight or reps each session."

- **Analysis**: What's happening
- **Numbers**: Specific metrics
- **Action Plan**: What to do next

## Privacy & Data

### Your Data Stays on Your Device

🔒 **100% Local Processing**
- All AI analysis happens on your phone
- No data is sent to external servers
- No cloud processing or third-party AI services

🔒 **Secure Storage**
- Insights are cached locally using Hive (encrypted storage)
- Cache is cleared when you log out
- No personal data leaves your device

### What Data is Used?

The AI Coach analyzes:
- Workout history (exercises, sets, reps, weights)
- Nutrition logs (meals, macros, calories)
- Body measurements (weight, measurements)
- Timestamps (to calculate trends and consistency)

**What is NOT used:**
- Personal information (name, email, etc.)
- Location data
- Device information
- Usage patterns outside the app

## Tips for Best Results

### 1. Log Consistently

The more data you provide, the better the insights:
- **Workouts**: Log every session with accurate weights and reps
- **Nutrition**: Track meals daily for accurate macro analysis
- **Body Metrics**: Weigh yourself weekly at the same time

### 2. Set Accurate Targets

Make sure your nutrition targets match your goals:
- **Muscle Gain**: Slight calorie surplus + high protein
- **Fat Loss**: Moderate calorie deficit + high protein
- **Maintenance**: Balanced calories + adequate protein

### 3. Act on Insights

Insights are only valuable if you use them:
- **Read the action plan** carefully
- **Implement recommendations** in your next session
- **Track progress** to see if changes are working

### 4. Be Patient

Meaningful insights require data:
- **Week 1**: Basic tracking reminders
- **Week 2-3**: Consistency patterns emerge
- **Week 4+**: Trend analysis and personalized recommendations

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How often do insights update?

**A:** Insights are cached for 1 hour and update automatically when:
- You complete a workout
- You log a meal
- You record body weight
- The cache expires (1 hour)

### Q: Why am I seeing "No insights yet"?

**A:** This means you need more data. Start by:
1. Logging your first workout
2. Tracking your meals for a few days
3. Recording your body weight

After 3-4 days of consistent logging, you'll start seeing personalized insights.

### Q: Can I customize which insights I see?

**A:** Currently, the AI Coach automatically prioritizes the most important insights for you. Future updates may include customization options.

### Q: Why did my insight disappear?

**A:** Insights update based on your latest data. If you addressed a recommendation (e.g., trained a neglected muscle group), that insight will be replaced with a new one.

### Q: How accurate are the insights?

**A:** Insights are based on proven fitness principles:
- Progressive overload for strength gains
- Protein intake for muscle recovery
- Consistency for long-term results
- Balanced training for injury prevention

However, they're general recommendations. Consult a professional for personalized medical or training advice.

### Q: What if I disagree with an insight?

**A:** The AI Coach provides recommendations based on data patterns, but you know your body best. Feel free to:
- Adjust recommendations to fit your situation
- Consult a trainer or nutritionist
- Continue with your current approach if it's working

### Q: Does the AI Coach replace a personal trainer?

**A:** No. The AI Coach is a tool to help you track progress and stay consistent. It cannot:
- Teach proper exercise form
- Provide medical advice
- Create fully customized programs
- Adapt to injuries or health conditions

Consider it a supplement to, not a replacement for, professional guidance.

## Troubleshooting

### Insights Not Updating

**Try these steps:**
1. Pull down to refresh the screen
2. Log out and log back in
3. Check that you've logged recent data
4. Wait a few minutes for cache to update

### Quick Feedback Not Showing

**Check:**
1. Did you complete a full workout?
2. Did you finish the workout (not just exit)?
3. Is the app up to date?

### Seeing Generic Insights

**This means:**
- You need more data for personalized insights
- Keep logging consistently for 1-2 weeks
- Generic insights will be replaced with personalized ones
