# Notifications & Reminders - User Guide

## How to Set Up Reminders

### 1. Access Reminder Settings

1. Open IronFlow app
2. Tap **Profile** tab (bottom navigation)
3. Tap **Reminder Settings**

---

### 2. Enable Workout Reminders

**Purpose**: Get reminded to train every day

**Steps**:
1. Toggle **"Workout Reminders"** ON
2. Tap the time to change it (default: 7:00 AM)
3. Select your preferred time
4. Tap **"Done"** or **"OK"**

**Result**: You'll receive a notification every day at your chosen time:
> 💪 **Time to Train!**  
> Your workout is waiting. Let's get stronger today!

---

### 3. Enable Meal Reminders

**Purpose**: Remember to log your meals for nutrition tracking

**Steps**:
1. Toggle **"Meal Reminders"** ON
2. Set times for:
   - **Breakfast** (default: 8:00 AM)
   - **Lunch** (default: 12:00 PM)
   - **Dinner** (default: 6:00 PM)
3. Tap each time to customize

**Result**: You'll receive 3 notifications daily:

**Breakfast**:
> 🍳 **Breakfast Time!**  
> Log your breakfast to track your nutrition

**Lunch**:
> 🍱 **Lunch Time!**  
> Log your lunch to track your nutrition

**Dinner**:
> 🍽️ **Dinner Time!**  
> Log your dinner to track your nutrition

---

### 4. Enable Streak Reminders

**Purpose**: Maintain your workout consistency streak

**Steps**:
1. Toggle **"Streak Reminders"** ON
2. Tap the time to change it (default: 9:00 PM)
3. Select your preferred time
4. Tap **"Done"** or **"OK"**

**Result**: You'll receive a notification every day at your chosen time:
> 🔥 **Keep Your Streak!**  
> Don't break your workout streak. Train today!

---

### 5. Test Notifications

**Purpose**: Verify notifications are working

**Steps**:
1. Scroll to bottom of Reminder Settings
2. Tap **"Test Notification"** button
3. Check if notification appears

**Result**: You should see:
> 🔔 **Test Notification**  
> This is a test notification from IronFlow!

---

## Notification Permissions

### First Time Setup

When you first enable reminders, you'll be asked for permission:

**Android**:
- Tap **"Allow"** when prompted
- If you accidentally denied, go to:
  - Settings → Apps → IronFlow → Notifications → Enable

**iOS**:
- Tap **"Allow"** when prompted
- If you accidentally denied, go to:
  - Settings → IronFlow → Notifications → Enable

## Disabling Reminders

### Temporarily Disable

1. Go to Reminder Settings
2. Toggle the reminder OFF
3. Notification cancelled immediately

### Permanently Disable

1. Go to device Settings
2. Apps → IronFlow → Notifications
3. Disable all notifications

## Troubleshooting

### "I'm not receiving notifications"

**Check these**:
1. ✅ Reminder enabled in app?
2. ✅ Correct time set?
3. ✅ Permissions granted?
4. ✅ Phone not in Do Not Disturb mode?
5. ✅ Battery optimization disabled for IronFlow?

**Android specific**:
- Settings → Apps → IronFlow → Battery → Unrestricted

**iOS specific**:
- Settings → IronFlow → Notifications → Allow Notifications

---

### "Notifications stopped after phone restart"

**This shouldn't happen** - notifications automatically reschedule after reboot.

**If it does**:
1. Open IronFlow app once
2. Notifications will reschedule
3. Check permissions are still granted

---

### "Test notification doesn't work"

**Try**:
1. Close and reopen the app
2. Check notification permissions
3. Try enabling a reminder and wait for scheduled time

---

### "Notifications appear but no sound"

**Check**:
1. Phone not in silent mode?
2. Notification sound enabled in settings?
3. Volume turned up?

**Android**:
- Settings → Apps → IronFlow → Notifications → Workout Reminders → Sound

**iOS**:
- Settings → IronFlow → Notifications → Sounds

---

## Tips for Best Results

### 1. Set Realistic Times
- Choose times when you're actually available
- Don't set workout reminder for 5:00 AM if you never wake up that early

### 2. Use Multiple Reminders
- Enable both workout AND streak reminders
- Workout reminder = morning motivation
- Streak reminder = evening check-in

### 3. Adjust as Needed
- Change times based on your schedule
- Disable on rest days if needed (manually)

### 4. Combine with Habits
- Set meal reminders for actual meal times
- Use as cue to open app and log data

### 5. Don't Ignore Them
- If you consistently ignore reminders, your brain learns to ignore them
- Only enable reminders you'll actually act on

**Yes!** Notifications are completely optional:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Will notifications drain my battery?
**A**: No. Notifications use the system alarm manager which is very efficient. Battery impact is negligible.

### Q: Can I customize notification sounds?
**A**: Currently no, but this is a planned feature. For now, uses system default sound.

### Q: Can I snooze notifications?
**A**: Not yet, but this is a planned feature.

### Q: Will notifications work offline?
**A**: Yes! Notifications are scheduled locally and don't require internet.

### Q: Can I set different times for weekdays vs weekends?
**A**: Not yet, but this is a planned feature.

### Q: How many notifications will I receive per day?
**A**: Depends on what you enable:
- Workout only: 1 per day
- Meals only: 3 per day
- Streak only: 1 per day
- All enabled: 5 per day

### Q: Can I disable specific meal reminders?
**A**: Currently no - it's all or nothing for meal reminders. This may be added in future.
