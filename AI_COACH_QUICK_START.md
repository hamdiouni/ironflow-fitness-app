# AI Fitness Coach - Quick Start Guide

## How to Access

1. **Open the app**
2. **Navigate to Profile tab** (bottom navigation)
3. **Tap "AI Fitness Coach"** (first item in settings)
4. **Start chatting!**

---

## What Can You Ask?

### 💪 Workout Questions
- "How's my training going?"
- "Am I training enough?"
- "What exercises should I focus on?"
- "Am I making progress?"

### 🍽️ Nutrition Questions
- "Should I change my diet?"
- "Am I eating enough protein?"
- "How are my macros?"
- "What should I eat more of?"

### 📈 Progress Questions
- "Analyze my progress"
- "How much have I improved?"
- "Am I gaining weight?"
- "Show me my results"

### 💡 General Questions
- "What should I focus on next?"
- "I need motivation"
- "Give me advice"
- "What's my status?"

## Quick Actions

Tap any quick action button at the top:

1. **Generate Workout** - Get workout program suggestions
2. **Adjust Diet** - Get nutrition recommendations
3. **Analyze Progress** - See detailed progress analysis
4. **What's Next?** - Get personalized next steps

## Troubleshooting

### "No recent workouts logged"
**Solution**: Log at least one workout first

### "No nutrition data logged yet"
**Solution**: Track at least one meal first

### "No user authenticated"
**Solution**: Make sure you're logged in

### Error message appears
**Solution**: Tap "Dismiss" and try again

## Privacy & Data

### What Data Does It Use?
- Your workout history (last 30 days)
- Your nutrition logs (last 7 days)
- Your body weight entries (last 30 days)
- Your user profile (goals, fitness level)

### Where Is Data Stored?
- **Locally on your device only**
- No data sent to external servers
- No API calls to third parties
- Complete privacy

### Can I Delete My Data?
- Yes! Use "Clear Chat History" button
- Or clear all app data in settings
