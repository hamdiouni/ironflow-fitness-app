## 💡 Why These Extreme Differences?

### Web Browser Limitation:
- Web browsers (Chrome, Firefox) have **limited TTS voices**
- Often only 1-2 voices available
- Voice selection doesn't work well
- **Solution**: Use EXTREME pitch and speed differences instead

### Mobile Advantage:
- iOS and Android have **many voices**
- Voice selection works perfectly
- Pitch and speed differences are MORE noticeable
- **Result**: Even better experience on mobile!
